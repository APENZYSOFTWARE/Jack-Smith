📋 Image Guidelines

  • Use clean, minimalist backgrounds (white, light gray, or subtle gradients)
  • Ensure products are well-lit with soft, even lighting
  • Include multiple angles: front, back, side, and detail shots
  • Maintain consistent style across all product categories
  • Use bold, sparkling colors that match your brand theme
  • Optimize for web: 800x800px for main images, 400x400px for thumbnails
  • Include lifestyle shots showing products in use
  • Ensure accurate color representation
  • File formats: JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency
